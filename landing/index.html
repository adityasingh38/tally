Frequently asked questions.

How does Tally capture my transactions automatically?
On Android, Tally listens for incoming transaction alert notifications dispatched by your bank or UPI apps. It processes the text on-device to extract the merchant and amount, and discards everything else.
Does Tally read my OTPs or personal WhatsApp messages?
Never. Tally uses a strict allowlist regex filter targeting only certified bank and payment sender IDs. Non-financial messages and messages containing OTP authentication tokens are immediately filtered out and ignored before any data processing takes place.
Is Tally free, and how do you make money?
Tally offers a generous free tier with automatic parsing and monthly receipts. A Tally Pro subscription provides unlimited real-time category insights, custom damage alerts, and historical export options. We never monetize by pushing credit card or loan offers.
