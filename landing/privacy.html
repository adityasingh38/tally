Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: 9 JUNE 2026 • LAST UPDATED: 14 NOVEMBER 2026

Tally ("Tally", "we", "us") is a personal expense-tracking mobile app that reads transactional alerts to automatically log your spending and show you honest financial insights. This policy explains what we collect, why, how we handle it, and your rights.

1. Who We Are

Tally is operated by Aditya Singh, an individual developer based in India.
Contact: adityasuper38@gmail.com.

2. What We Collect

a) Account Data

Email address and authentication credentials (handled securely by our auth provider, Supabase). Used solely to create, authenticate, and secure your account.

b) Transaction Data

Tally parses transaction alerts only from known bank and UPI senders. From those alerts, we extract: transaction amount, type (debit/credit), merchant name, masked account/card digits, date/time, and sender identifier.

On-Device Parsing Guarantee

The raw body of your messages and notifications is parsed entirely on your device and is NEVER uploaded or stored on our servers. Only the extracted transaction fields (amount, merchant, category, date) are stored in your secure account. Personal, non-bank messages are filtered out locally and never read or processed.

c) App Data You Provide

Monthly budgets, category corrections, and display preferences. Preferences are stored locally on your device; budgets and categorised transactions are stored in your private account.

d) Subscription Data

If you purchase Tally Pro, subscription status is managed by Google Play Billing via RevenueCat. We never receive, process, or store your credit card or banking credentials.

We do NOT collect: contacts, photos, precise GPS location, browser history, or call logs.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To automatically log, categorise, and organise your spending transactions.
  • To display spending trends, budget thresholds, and alerts before you overspend.
  • To generate AI-assisted categorisation and monthly spending verdicts.
  • To operate your account, provide customer support, and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your information for targeted advertising.

4. AI Processing

To categorise ambiguous transactions and generate spending commentary, limited transaction context (such as the merchant name and amount) is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, via server-side API. This data is processed transiently and is never used to build advertising profiles.

5. Permissions We Request (Android)

  • Notifications / Notification Listener Service: Used to capture transactional bank alerts in real time so transactions appear automatically with zero manual entry.
  • SMS (READ_SMS / RECEIVE_SMS): Used on supported configurations to parse transactional bank SMS locally. You may decline and use manual entry or notification capture.
  • Post Notifications & Vibrate: To send you budget alerts and weekly spending summaries.

6. Where Your Data Is Stored

Account and transaction records are stored with Supabase (managed PostgreSQL) with Row-Level Security (RLS) enabled. All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS / TLS 1.3.

7. Data Sharing

We share data exclusively with trusted infrastructure service providers required to deliver the service:

  • Supabase — Database hosting, authentication, and encrypted storage.
  • Anthropic — Server-side AI categorisation and financial commentary.
  • RevenueCat & Google Play — In-app subscription validation.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data with any data broker or advertiser.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

  • We keep transaction records while your account remains active.
  • One-tap account deletion: You can permanently delete your entire account and all associated transaction records at any time directly in the app (Settings → Delete account).
  • Upon deletion, your data is cascaded and permanently purged from our servers.

9. Your Rights & Grievance Redressal

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and applicable privacy regulations, you have the right to access, rectify, and erase your personal data.

For any privacy requests or grievances, contact our Grievance Officer directly:

  • Grievance Officer: Aditya Singh
  • Email: adityasuper38@gmail.com

10. Security

We use industry-standard encryption in transit, strict database row-level security, and client-side alert parsing to minimise data footprint. While no system is impenetrable, we actively maintain safeguards to protect your personal information.
